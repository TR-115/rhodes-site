About Us

Rhodes Outdoor Equipment is a small gear company in Victoria, BC, started by Tyler Rhodes. After one too many hikes spent hitching his rifle sling back onto his shoulder, Tyler went looking for a fix. Local shops didn't carry one. Online, the options were either pricey US imports or poorly made versions that didn't look like they'd last.

So he made his own and took it on a hunt. The difference it made was so big that he knew other hunters would want one too. That prototype became the Sling Hook.
